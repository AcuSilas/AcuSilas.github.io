Clustering maize markets by price behaviour

About

Data work in service of decisions

I'm Silas, a geospatial engineer and data scientist based in Nairobi. I trained in geospatial engineering at the University of Nairobi and moved through GIS and data-analyst roles into public-health research — where I spend most of my time now, turning routine surveillance and spatial data into things malaria programmes can act on.

Across public health and agriculture, the common thread is the same: clean, reproducible pipelines that other people can run and trust. I care about documentation, graceful error handling, and analysis that ends in a clear decision rather than a chart. When I'm not doing that, I'm usually building small software tools for myself.
